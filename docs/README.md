# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation. This collection provides comprehensive guidance for how we run projects, coordinate teams, and deliver value to customers.

## Overview of OctoAcme Project Management

OctoAcme operates a structured, lifecycle-based approach to project management that spans from initial concept through post-delivery retrospectives. Projects flow through five distinct phases: **Initiation** (validating business need and aligning stakeholders), **Planning** (breaking work into shippable increments), **Execution** (daily delivery with quality gates), **Release** (standardized deployment with rollback plans), and **Close & Retrospective** (capturing learnings). At each phase, lightweight but rigorous deliverables—from Project One-pagers to risk registers—ensure transparency and alignment across stakeholders.

The delivery model relies on three core roles with distinct responsibilities: **Project Managers** coordinate schedules, risks, and communications to keep projects on track; **Product Managers** define what to build by prioritizing the backlog and measuring customer impact; and **Developers** (supported by QA/Testing) implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards. This clear ownership model prevents silos and enables rapid decision-making.

Communication is structured through a consistent cadence—daily standups for blockers and progress, weekly syncs between PM and Product Lead, twice-weekly team standups, and monthly stakeholder updates—ensuring no surprises and early escalation of risks. Quality and testing are embedded throughout execution via small reviewable pull requests (≤400 lines), automated CI tests and security scans, and end-to-end smoke tests before release.

OctoAcme treats retrospectives as a formal process, converting insights into prioritized action items with clear owners and due dates. This commitment to continuous improvement, combined with transparent risk tracking and structured communication, creates a predictable, learning-oriented delivery culture that scales across cross-functional teams.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first:** prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery:** deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership:** each project has named accountability
- **Data-informed decisions:** measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety:** encourage feedback and learning
